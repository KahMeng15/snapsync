Engineered For Events

Everything Built For Live Engagements

Reliable photobooth software that puts your branding front and center.

DSLR or Webcam Ready

Connect tethered Canon, Sony, or Nikon cameras via USB for studio clarity, or use any standard webcam for rapid, zero-friction deployments.

Custom Branding Overlays

Easily apply custom PNG frame graphics, sponsor logos, event hashtags, and date banners. Turn every photo into a branded digital keepsake.

Instant QR Code Delivery

Attendees scan a clean QR code on the booth display right after posing to immediately view and download their high-resolution photos.

Self-Hosted & Private

No subscription lock-in, no per-event fees, and no third-party watermarks. All media is hosted locally and remains under your direct control.

Operator Hub

Manage photo sessions, preview live captures, monitor printer queues, and export high-res archives from any device on your local network.

Offline Resilient

Unreliable venue Wi-Fi won't interrupt your event. Captures are safely stored in a local offline queue and sync smoothly whenever ready.

Hardware Versatility

Choose Your Capture Setup

SnapSync adapts to standard USB webcams as well as professional tethered DSLRs.

Studio Setup

DSLR & Mirrorless Tethering

Direct USB control using gphoto2 on macOS and DigiCamControl on Windows.

  • Canon EOS (80D, 5D, R-series, Rebel series)
  • Sony Alpha (A7 III, A7 IV, A6400)
  • Optical live view and external strobe flash sync
Plug & Play

USB & Built-in Webcams

Instant zero-driver setup using standard browser and system media APIs.

  • Logitech Brio, C920, OBSBOT, and standard USB webcams
  • MacBook & laptop integrated cameras
  • Ideal for quick pop-ups, hackathons, and small venues
Quick Start

How It Works

A simple, reliable workflow from connection to guest sharing.

01

Connect Camera

Connect your camera via USB. Launch the client application and choose your camera mode.

02

Load Custom Branding Overlays

Upload your event frames and logos in the operator hub to brand every photo capture.

03

Capture & QR Share

Guests tap Start, pose for the countdown, and scan the on-screen QR code to download their photos.
